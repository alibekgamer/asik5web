Interesting Dinosaur Facts

Simple facts that make the prehistoric world even more interesting.

5 Things to Remember
  1. Dinosaurs lived millions of years ago.
  2. Different dinosaurs had very different diets.
  3. Some dinosaurs were herbivores and some were carnivores.
  4. Fossils help scientists study prehistoric life.
  5. Birds are considered living dinosaurs by modern science.
Why Fossils Matter
  • Fossils preserve evidence of ancient life.
  • Bones can show body structure.
  • Footprints can give clues about movement.
  • Fossil discoveries help scientists build new theories.

Life in the Mesozoic Era

The Mesozoic Era is often called the Age of Reptiles. It included the Triassic, Jurassic and Cretaceous periods. During this huge span of time, dinosaurs became one of the most important groups of land animals.
